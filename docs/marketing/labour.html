The door

Scan in, scan out

Any phone at the pack-house door. Tap Scan Worker QR, point the camera at the badge, and the screen fills with IN or OUT, the name and the time. The next worker is one tap away.

  • The phone decides IN or OUT from who is inside, so a worker never has to choose. The operator can flip it while the screen is still up.
  • Works with no signal. Scans are saved on the phone with the time they were made and sent when the connection returns. "Waiting to sync" counts them.
  • Every door phone in one list. Each phone has a name; a phone that went quiet while the others kept scanning is easy to spot.
  • Forgot to scan out? An OUT long after the IN is flagged on the screen, with the time of that IN, while the operator can still change it.
  • Badges from the register. The QR badge is the one Plaashek Harvest prints; a badge that is not an active worker is refused by name.
  • No pay on the door phone. It shows who is inside and when they came in, never hours or money.
Admin

Who is inside, and what needs fixing

The admin page is behind a PIN, on the admin's own phone. Today shows who is in the building right now. Fix lists every scan without a partner: those hours are not paid until someone fixes them, with a reason that stays on record.

  • Nothing paid by guesswork. An IN with no OUT, or an OUT with no IN, is an exception, not a shift. The pay report says how many are outstanding.
  • Every correction on record: what changed, when, from which phone and why. Deleted scans are kept, marked deleted.
  • Night shifts are fine. Up to 18 hours counts as one shift; longer is flagged as a forgotten exit.
  • No future times. A time in the future is a wrong date, and the form says so instead of saving it.
Admin

Pay and settings

Pick this week, last week or any dates, tap Show, and every worker's days, hours, overtime hours and amount are there. Export downloads the wage sheet, the shifts behind it and the unresolved scans as three sheets of one spreadsheet.

  • Overtime the BCEA way. Hours past the daily threshold are overtime; of the rest, hours that take the Monday-to-Sunday week past the weekly threshold are overtime on the day they happen. Never double counted.
  • Rates are dated, never edited. A new rate applies from its date; earlier days keep the rate they had, so last month's sheet never changes.
  • Whole weeks for the threshold. A pay period that starts on a Thursday still knows about Monday's hours.
  • Nightly backups of every scan, correction and rate, kept for 30 days, with Backup now.
  • PIN, not passwords. The door phones need none; the admin page needs the PIN, and changing it signs every other phone out.
