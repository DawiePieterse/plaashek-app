DevicesAny Apple or Android phone or tablet, added to the home screen like an app. For the picking stations we recommend the rugged Ulefone RugKing 3 Pro or 4 Pro.
ModulesSwitched on per farm, charged monthly, added or removed by a message. Weer and Eienaar are free and always on.
