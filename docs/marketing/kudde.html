Field

The herd, camp by camp

The field app is for whoever is out with the cattle. The herd is listed by camp in the farm's own order, each row with the animal's class, when it was last weighed, and its weight. Search by tag or name, or scan the tag.

  • Works with no signal. Every record is saved on the phone first and sent when the server is reachable. Counts are worked out on the phone, so they are right the moment something is recorded.
  • Six events. Birth, weight, treatment, movement, sale and death. A death or sale sets the animal's status; there is no separate field to forget.
  • Class is derived. Cow, heifer, bull, steer or calf comes from sex, birth date, the castrated flag and whether she has calved.
  • Need weighing is a worklist. Tap the count and the list becomes only the animals overdue, by camp.
Field

Recording an event

Today or yesterday with one tap, or any past date. A weight shows the last one and the daily gain. A treatment remembers the farm's products and sets the withdrawal period, so the animal is flagged until the day it may be sold.

  • Sessions for the crush. A weigh or treat session takes one animal after another: type or scan the tag, the number, Save and next. A treat session applies the same product and dose to each.
  • Calvings in one step. Calved on a cow records the birth and creates the calf with its dam and sire, in the cow's camp.
  • Weather for free. With the farm's position set, each event is stamped with that day's temperature and rain, for looking back later.
  • Camps snap to spelling. A camp typed at the gate matches the office's spelling, capitals or not; an unknown one is added rather than refused.
Admin

The office view

The dashboard counts the live herd by class and by camp, lists every animal overdue a weighing and every one still in withdrawal, and shows the latest events from every phone.

  • Withdrawal is never missed. Any animal inside a treatment's withdrawal is listed with the date it clears, and flagged in red on the herd list.
  • Six months without a weight puts an animal on the needs-weighing list, with a link straight to its record.
Admin

Herd, records and settings

  • Export for the accountant or the vet. The herd and every event as spreadsheets.
  • Parents need not be on file. A bought-in animal or a borrowed bull can still be a sire or dam.
  • Not only cattle. Kudde is adjusted for sheep, goats, pigs and other livestock: the classes (ewe, ram, lamb; sow, boar, piglet), weigh intervals, gestation and litters are the farm's own settings. Speak to Plaashek about your situation.
Other animalsSheep, goats, pigs, game or any other livestock: Kudde is set up for your animals with their own class names and rules. Speak to Plaashek about your situation.
