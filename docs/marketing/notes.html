Capture

Say it where you see it

A note is a title, the block or spot, the words, the actions it leads to, tags and photos. Tap the microphone on the phone's keyboard and talk; the app stamps where you stood and what the weather was.

  • Offline first. Save Note writes to the phone at once and syncs in the background every few seconds while there is signal. No note is ever lost to a dead spot in the orchard.
  • Captured once, never changed. Location and weather are recorded when the note is made; an edit at the house that evening does not move it.
  • Actions, not just words. Tap Prune, Fertilise, Water, Spray, Scout or Pick, say with what, and it goes on the To do list. Tick the circle if it is already done.
  • Tags you keep adding to. A starter set of farm tags, and any word typed becomes a new one. Spelling snaps to the existing tag.
  • Photos from the camera roll or taken on the spot, several per note.
  • Afrikaans and English alike. Search ignores accents and case, so "spuit" finds "Spuitprogram" and "more" finds "môre".
  • Apple or Android, added to the home screen like an app, in the phone's own light or dark mode.
To do

From observation to work order to history

The To do list is the farm's work-order book. Every action still waiting, grouped by block, longest waiting first: what to do, with what, and by when. The foreman opens it on their phone, does the job, taps the circle and says what was actually used. The note then records that the block was pruned, or sprayed, on which day and with what.

  • Work orders for staff. A note written by the owner becomes a job the foreman or a team leader sees on their own phone, with the block, the instruction and the product and rate. No paper slip, no WhatsApp message to find again.
  • Later. The calendar on a row gives a job a day, tomorrow, in a week or a date, and parks it until then. The tab badge counts only what is due.
  • Works with no signal. A tick made in the orchard is dated when it was ticked, not when it synced.
  • Two phones, one notebook. Refresh pulls in what the other phone has added.
Notes and Dashboard

Find it again

Search by title, text or block, and filter by tag, block, variety or action. Choose a block and an action together and you have that block's history: every spraying of 8b, when, and with what.

  • Fifty at a time. The list loads as it is scrolled, however many seasons are in the book.
  • Archive, never delete. An archived note disappears from every list but is kept, and Restore puts it back as it was.
A note in full, and Settings

Everything a note knows

  • Blocks with a variety. Set up once; the Capture screen groups them by type, and Notes can filter by either.
  • Optional AI help. With a key on the server, AI Tidy up punctuates a dictated note, suggests tags and the actions it implies, and Ask AI answers questions from the notebook alone. Without a key, everything else works.
  • Backups every night, database and photos, with Back Up Now.
  • No audio stored. Dictation is the phone's own; only the words reach the app.
Who it is forThe owner and whoever will run the farm after them, and the staff who carry out the work orders on the To do list. A phone each, one notebook, no sign-in.
Hosting and dataYour farm lives at yourfarm.plaashek.co.za on a South African hosting service, encrypted in transit and at rest, backed up daily, never shared between farms.
