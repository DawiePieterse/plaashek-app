The rest of the suite

Other Plaashek modules

The modules in this document are the ones built and running on a working farm today. Plaashek is sold as modules per farm, all working on one set of the farm's records, so each of the modules below reads what the others already hold rather than asking for it again. Two are free and always on.

Free, always on

Weer

Weather

The forecast for the farm's own position, now and the next 16 days; the rain record for the last day, week, month and the season against every year since 1940; frost nights highlighted; any year against any other on one chart. Its daily weather record is what the other modules stamp their captures with.

Free, always on

Eienaar

Owner

The first module a farm has after setup: a summary of what is happening on the farm, drawn from whichever modules are in use, with a "needs attention" list (a withdrawal ending, a weighing due, a frost night forecast) and optional AI analytics in plain language.

Module

Werkers

Workers and labour

The full worker register: identity, employment, bank and tax details, encrypted. Time and attendance per team (the Labour time clock in this document lives here), contractor crews and rates, payroll and wage runs with payslips, PAYE and UIF, bank and cash lists. Harvest's piece-rate pay flows into it.

Module

Veldnotas

Field notes

The Notes module in this document, in the suite: farm information captured anywhere as photo, voice or text, kept searchable, with work orders for staff and AI trend analysis across seasons.

Module

Stoor

Input stores

Chemical and fertiliser stock on hand, issues per block, linked to the spray programme, with expiry dates and safety data sheets. The office knows what is on the shelf without walking out to count it, and where it went.

Module

Water

Irrigation and boreholes

Borehole, meter and level readings, pump hours and scheduling, water use per block and per season. The latest reading per point and how much moved since the last visit, without a paper logbook in a bakkie.

Module

Bespuitings

Spray records

Spray and fertigation records per block, product and operator, with the withholding period and safe-harvest date worked out, booked against Stoor's stock and Water's readings. The paper compliance book, kept by the phone.

Module

Werkswinkel

Implements and vehicles

Service schedules and job cards for every tractor, pump and bakkie, breakdown and repair history, diesel and hours per machine.

Module

Oudit

Compliance

GlobalG.A.P., SIZA and buyer audit evidence assembled from Harvest, Kudde and Stoor records, with gaps flagged before audit week.

Any crop, any process

Your module

Built for your farm

A process the suite does not cover yet, from grading to cold store to a weigh sheet of your own, is added as a screen with the same badge sign-in, offline capture and reports. Speak to Plaashek about your situation.

Each module is switched on per farm and charged monthly. Weer and Eienaar are free and are never switched off. Hosting is per farm, in South Africa, at yourfarm.plaashek.co.za.
